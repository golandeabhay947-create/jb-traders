Diagnostic Guide

Engine Symptom & Lubrication Solver

Engine Overheating Under Load

Low shear-stability oil breaks down under high temperatures, reducing oil film thickness and increasing metal friction.

Fix: Upgrade to Motocoat / Finolube 15W-40 CI-4 or Synthetic 5W-30 with high flash-point base stock.
Black Smoke & Rapid Oil Burning

Volatile mineral base oils evaporate through piston rings into the combustion chamber, forming valve carbon.

Fix: Switch to low-NOACK synthetic blend engine oil with high TBN detergent package.
Tractor Wet Brake Chatter Noise

Standard engine oils lack frictional modifiers required for submerged oil-bath disc brakes.

Fix: Use dedicated J B Traders UTTO 15W40 specifically formulated for chatter-free braking.
